SQF Practitioner Training Course

The official SQFI-recognized training course for anyone who will serve as a designated SQF Practitioner at their facility. This is not optional — SQF requires every certified facility to have a trained, registered Practitioner on file.

1

Who This Is For

Anyone designated as the SQF Practitioner at their facility. This is the person responsible for building, implementing, and maintaining the food safety management system. Required before your first SQF audit.

2

What's Covered

The SQF Code structure, food safety plan development, HACCP integration, prerequisite programs, documentation requirements, internal audit preparation, and how to maintain certification year over year.

3

Format & Completion

Online, self-paced. Upon completion, practitioners are listed in the official SQFI Practitioner database. Recertification is required every 3 years.

SQF Internal Auditor Training

SQF certification requires regular internal audits — and the person conducting them needs to know what they're doing. This course trains your team to audit your own system rigorously so nothing surprises you on certification day.

1

Who This Is For

SQF Practitioners, quality managers, and food safety team members responsible for conducting internal audits. Strongly recommended for anyone involved in SQF compliance beyond day-to-day operations.

2

What's Covered

Audit planning and scheduling, conducting audit interviews and walkthroughs, writing audit findings, non-conformance reporting, corrective action follow-up, and maintaining audit records for SQF compliance.

3

Format & Completion

Online course with a certificate of completion. Recommended for anyone who will be responsible for the internal audit schedule at their facility.
