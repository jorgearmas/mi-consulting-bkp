HACCP Basic Certificate Program

A foundational HACCP certification course covering all seven principles. Designed for team members who need a solid working knowledge of HACCP — from line supervisors to anyone new to food safety documentation.

1

Who This Is For

Food safety team members, line supervisors, quality technicians, and anyone new to HACCP who needs foundational certification. Ideal as a starting point before pursuing SQF Practitioner training.

2

What's Covered

All 7 HACCP principles, prerequisite program requirements, hazard analysis methodology, critical control point identification, monitoring procedures, corrective actions, verification, and record-keeping requirements.

3

Format & Completion

Online and in-person options available. Upon completion you'll receive an industry-recognized HACCP certificate accepted by SQF, BRC, and regulatory bodies.

Advanced HACCP & Food Safety Management

For practitioners who are building or auditing HACCP plans and need a deeper understanding of how HACCP integrates with SQF, FSMA, and modern food safety management systems.

1

Who This Is For

Quality managers, SQF Practitioners, food safety team leaders, and anyone responsible for writing, reviewing, or auditing HACCP plans at their facility. Assumes basic HACCP knowledge.

2

What's Covered

Advanced hazard analysis methodology, CCP vs. oPRP determination, HACCP plan validation and verification, how HACCP integrates with SQF Code requirements and FSMA Preventive Controls, and managing HACCP in a digital food safety platform.

3

Format & Completion

Online and in-person options available. Certificate valid 3 years. Recommended for anyone in a HACCP plan leadership role.
