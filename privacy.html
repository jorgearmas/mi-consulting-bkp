Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

MI Consulting Group LLC ("MI," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to information collected through our website at manufacturingintelligence.ai, our contact and training inquiry forms, and lead-generation forms we run on advertising platforms such as LinkedIn.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you choose to provide and a limited amount of technical information collected automatically.

Information you provide. When you submit our contact form, our training inquiry form, or a lead-generation form (for example, a LinkedIn Lead Gen Form used in our advertising campaigns), we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • The service tier or training course you are interested in
  • Any message you include

When you book a call with us, the scheduling provider collects the details you enter on its booking page.

SQF Readiness Quiz. The quiz on our home page runs in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us or stored.

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, our hosting provider may collect standard technical data such as pages viewed, referring source, browser type, device type, and country, through privacy-friendly web analytics (see Section 6).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and contact you about our SQF consulting, training, and advisory services;
  • Assess whether our services are a fit for your needs;
  • Provide, operate, and improve our services and website;
  • Measure and improve the performance of our website and marketing;
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to operate our business:

  • Service providers that help us run our website, forms, scheduling, and email, who process the data on our behalf. These include Formspree (form delivery), Vercel (website hosting and analytics), and Calendly (call scheduling).
  • Advertising platforms such as LinkedIn, when you submit a lead-generation form hosted on their platform, in accordance with their own privacy terms.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

4. Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, and meet our legal and business obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent. You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.

Third-party pages we link to, such as our scheduling page, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website and ads may link to third-party sites and platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be posted on this page.
