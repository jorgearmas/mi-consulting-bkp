Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

MI Consulting Group ("MI," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to information collected through our website at www.consultwithmi.com, our contact and qualification forms, and lead-generation forms we run on advertising platforms such as LinkedIn.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you choose to provide and a limited amount of technical information collected automatically.

Information you provide. When you submit our website contact form or a lead-generation form (for example, a LinkedIn Lead Gen Form used in our advertising campaigns), we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name and job details
  • City and state
  • Your best time to be contacted and any message or responses you provide

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we and our service providers may automatically collect standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring source, through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 6).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and contact you about SQF consulting and coaching services;
  • Assess whether our services are a fit for your needs;
  • Provide, operate, and improve our services and website;
  • Measure and improve the performance of our marketing and advertising campaigns;
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to operate our business:

  • Service providers that help us run our website, forms, email, and analytics (for example, our form-delivery provider and hosting provider), who process the data on our behalf.
  • Advertising platforms such as LinkedIn, when you submit a lead-generation form hosted on their platform, in accordance with their own privacy terms.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

4. Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, and meet our legal and business obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent. You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for functionality, analytics, and advertising, including:

  • Google Tag Manager, which manages the tags and scripts running on our site;
  • The LinkedIn Insight Tag, which helps us measure and optimize our LinkedIn advertising.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website and ads may link to third-party sites and platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be posted on this page.
